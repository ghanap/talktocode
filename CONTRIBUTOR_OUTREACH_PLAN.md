# Growth Strategy: Outreach Plan to Increase Contributor Base

This document outlines our strategic plan to rapidly increase the open-source contributor base for the Software Bug Assistant project.

## 1. Contributor Outreach Strategy & Onboarding
The biggest barrier to open-source contribution is the initial setup complexity. To lower this barrier:
*   **Clear Documentation:** We maintain a detailed `CONTRIBUTING.md` and utilize explicit issue templates (Bugs, Features, Docs) to standardize communication.
*   **"Good First Issues":** Maintainers will actively label low-complexity bugs and minor feature requests with `good first issue` and `help wanted`.
*   **Automated Linting:** We use pre-commit hooks (Ruff, MyPy) so contributors don't have to guess if their code meets our style guidelines.

## 2. Week-Wise Contributor Growth Plan
Our week-by-week approach to scaling our contributor community:
*   **Weeks 1-2 (Foundation):** Clean up the codebase, add `good first issue` tags, and finalize the `CONTRIBUTING.md` guidelines.
*   **Weeks 3-4 (Initial Outreach):** Post the repository in "Showoff Saturday" threads on Reddit (r/opensource, r/Python) and Hacker News. Launch a "Call for Contributors" on Dev.to.
*   **Weeks 5-8 (Mentorship & Hackathons):** Register the repository for global open-source events. Begin offering a 1-on-1 mentorship program for first-time contributors who get their first Pull Request merged.
*   **Weeks 9-12 (Community Building):** Launch a dedicated Discord server for contributors. Start a monthly "Contributor of the Month" spotlight on LinkedIn and Twitter.

## 3. Geographical Contributor Expansion Plans
To ensure a diverse and global contributor base, we will target specific regions horizontally:
*   **Targeting the Indian Tech Ecosystem:** Partnering with major Indian university tech clubs and open-source organizations to offer students hands-on experience contributing to a production-grade AI agent.
*   **LATAM Developer Communities:** Translating our `README.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md` into Spanish and Portuguese to lower the language barrier for developers in rapidly growing Latin American tech hubs.
*   **Global Hackathons:** Sponsoring and participating in geographically distributed hackathons (e.g., in Europe, APAC, and North America) to onboard contributors from different time zones, ensuring our project has 24/7 active community support.
