# Contributor Outreach & Growth Strategy

This document outlines our strategic plan to rapidly increase the open-source contributor base for the Software Bug Assistant project.

## 1. Frictionless Onboarding for New Contributors
The biggest barrier to open-source contribution is the initial setup complexity. To lower this barrier:
*   **Clear Documentation:** We maintain a detailed `CONTRIBUTING.md` and utilize explicit issue templates (Bugs, Features, Docs) to standardize communication.
*   **"Good First Issues":** Maintainers will actively label low-complexity bugs and minor feature requests with `good first issue` and `help wanted`. These will be reserved strictly for first-time contributors.
*   **Automated Linting:** We use pre-commit hooks (Ruff, MyPy) so contributors don't have to guess if their code meets our style guidelines—it is formatted for them automatically.

## 2. Active Outreach Channels
We will actively promote the project in spaces where developers look for contribution opportunities:
*   **Hacktoberfest & Open Source Events:** Register the repository in major open-source events to attract seasonal contributors.
*   **Developer Communities:** Regularly publish articles on platforms like Dev.to, Hashnode, and Medium detailing the architecture of the ADK agent, and append a "Call for Contributors" section at the end of each post.
*   **Social Amplification:** Use Twitter/X and LinkedIn to highlight significant merged PRs from external contributors to showcase community appreciation.

## 3. Contributor Retention & Incentives
Getting a developer to submit their first PR is good, but retaining them as a core contributor is our primary goal.
*   **Rapid PR Reviews:** Maintainers commit to reviewing external PRs within 48 hours to keep contributor momentum high.
*   **Public Recognition:** Active contributors will be permanently featured in the `README.md` under an "All Contributors" section.
*   **Mentorship Pipeline:** Exceptional contributors who consistently submit high-quality PRs will be offered "Maintainer" status and direct mentorship from the core team to help guide the project's architectural decisions.
